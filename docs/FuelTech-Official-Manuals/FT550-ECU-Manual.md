## Project use

Treat this as the primary manufacturer reference for the FT550 ECU, including:

- A and B connector assignments
- ECU power and ground requirements
- sensor 5 V supplies and sensor grounds
- crank and cam trigger inputs
- injector outputs
- ignition outputs
- auxiliary inputs and outputs
- CAN communications
- configuration and calibration requirements

For the Turbo V-Rod build, FuelTech pin and electrical requirements remain authoritative while the project-specific harness distributes them through the EPM, APM and SIM electrical domains.
